The zombie judges have spoken. Meet the frightfully creative winners and runners-up of Hall-O-Advocacy 2026!

THE JUDGES HAVE SPOKEN.
THE PETS HAVE LAWYERED UP.

OPAC's 3rd Annual Hall-O-Advocacy Winners!

WARNING: Frightening images, extraordinary creativity, and questionable judgment ahead. Physician discretion is advised.

Dear Fellow Survivors,

The votes are in, the judges have shuffled back from the grave, and while they may be a little brain-dead, they still know a winning costume when they see one.

After digging them up and reviewing a frightfully impressive collection of costumes, props, pets, and Halloween creativity, we are thrilled to unveil the winners of OPAC's 3rd Annual Hall-O-Advocacy Halloween Costume Contest.

And because the universe has a sense of humor, today is Friday the 13th! Yes, we chose the unluckiest day on the calendar to announce the luckiest costumes of the year. Our zombie judges unanimously approved the timing. Of course, one voted with a detached hand, so we’re still reviewing the paperwork.

From brilliantly detailed creations to laugh-out-loud entries and costumes that were almost scarier than a Medicare payment update, this year's submissions delivered spooky-season magic in all the best ways.

So dim the lights, cue the eerie music, and join us in celebrating this year's wickedly wonderful winners and runners-up!

Six Categories. $400 in Prizes.
Frightfully Fabulous Costumes.

The winning entry in each category receives an Amazon Gift Card.

Most Creative Costume(s)$100
Best Group / Practice Costumes$100
Scariest Costume$50
Funniest Costume(s)$50
Best Pet Costume$50
Best Child(ren)'s Costume(s)$50

Prizes are awarded to category winners only. Runners-up and special recognitions receive no gift card.

MOST CREATIVE COSTUME(S)

Winner: Jill Kruse | $100 Amazon Gift Card

Some people decorate their porches with pumpkins. Jill Kruse's entry apparently opened a portal to another dimension!

Bringing Pure Vessel and Hornet from Hollow Knight to life, this spectacular submission featured hauntingly detailed costumes, dramatic masks, and enough fog to make a haunted house jealous.

Our zombie judges gave this creation two thumbs up. Of course, it took us nearly 20 minutes to locate both thumbs, but that's beside the point.

Runner-Up: Entry Submitted by Christine Hart

Talk about a MAJOR AWARD!

Straight from A Christmas Story, this brilliant recreation of the legendary Leg Lamp quite literally lit up the competition!

It's glowing. It's glamorous. It's FRAGILE! (Must be Italian.)

Somewhere, Ralphie's father is shedding a tear of pride. Meanwhile, our judges are still debating whether the costume should receive an award or be plugged into the nearest electrical outlet.

BEST GROUP / PRACTICE COSTUMES

Winner: Entry Submitted by Haven Jacob | $100 Amazon Gift Card

We've got a GOLDEN TICKET!

The Willy Wonka and the Chocolate Factory crew, submitted by Haven Jacob, turned an ordinary gathering into a full-blown candy-coated spectacle! From oversized lollipops to Oompa Loompas, Willy Wonka, and the coveted Golden Ticket, this group delivered enough creativity to give the entire chocolate factory a sugar high.

Our zombie judges were particularly impressed by the Golden Ticket. They've spent years searching for one that guarantees better Medicare reimbursement. Unfortunately, all they keep finding are chocolate bars and disappointment.

Runner-Up: Entry Submitted by Valerie McAuley

There's no place like home! There's no place like home!

But apparently, there's also no place like the Wizard of Oz crew, submitted by Valerie McAuley, who followed the yellow brick road straight into our runners-up spotlight!

With colorful characters, an Emerald City welcome, and one wickedly good witch, this group proved that a little brains, heart, and courage can go a long way.

Speaking of brains, heart, and courage... if anyone happens to find the Wizard, could you please send him to Capitol Hill? We have a few requests.

SCARIEST COSTUME

Winner: Entry Submitted by Mary McFarland | $50 Amazon Gift Card

HOLY MOTHER OF MEDICARE!

Just when we thought we had seen the worst horrors imaginable, Mary McFarland submitted Nightmare Fuel, a masterpiece of terrifying special-effects makeup that had our zombie judges questioning whether they had wandered into an actual horror movie.

The attention to detail was horrifying. The execution was flawless. And the judges? One screamed, another fainted, and the third tried to schedule an emergency dermatology appointment.

We have seen some frightening things in Washington, but this may be the first time we have looked at something and thought, “Maybe the Medicare Physician Fee Schedule isn't so scary after all.”

Runner-Up: Entry Submitted by Yasmine Awayes

Some monsters jump out of closets. Others hide under beds.

And then there is The Silent Haunt, submitted by Yasmine Awayes, which apparently prefers to lurk beneath trees in broad daylight, silently waiting for unsuspecting victims.

No screaming. No chasing. Just a towering, faceless nightmare that looks like it has been wandering the earth for centuries.

Our judges could not decide what was more disturbing: the costume itself or the fact that it was photographed in broad daylight! One judge reportedly asked whether the creature was available to accompany OPAC on its next visit to Capitol Hill.

FUNNIEST COSTUME(S)

Winner: Entry Submitted by Aubree Tannehill | $50 Amazon Gift Card

“I speak for the trees!” But this particular Lorax has expanded the portfolio. Now it is The Lorax: I Speak for the DOs!

Stationed outside an osteopathic medical school in a magnificent blaze of orange, this costume had our judges laughing so hard they nearly needed an OMT appointment.

We are not saying the Lorax should testify before Congress about Medicare reimbursement. We are merely saying the mustache has a certain gravitas.

Runner-Up: Entry Submitted by Ellie Cumpton

Dr. Evil and Mini-Me have arrived, and the plan for world domination is progressing nicely. First demand: ONE MILLION DOLLARS!

Our judges loved the commitment to character, the coordinated wardrobe, and the fact that Mini-Me may be the more intimidating negotiator.

If this duo ever visits Capitol Hill, we recommend listening to their demands. The alternative might involve an unnecessarily dramatic swivel chair.

BEST PET COSTUME

Winner: Entry Submitted by Eva Morrison | $50 Amazon Gift Card

101 Spots of Style!

Some contestants brought a costume. Eva Morrison brought an entire fashion statement on horseback! The spotted horse and coordinated rider delivered a look that would make Cruella de Vil abandon her plans and open a riding academy instead.

Our judges awarded extra points for style, teamwork, and successfully convincing a horse that this was all a perfectly reasonable way to spend Halloween.

Runner-Up: Entry Submitted by Emma Geister

And now, an important question: who looked at a bearded dragon and thought, “You know what this needs? Mustard.”

Hot Diggity Dragon arrived in full hot-dog glory, proving that even a reptile can be the life of the cookout.

The judges were delighted. The dragon has declined to comment and is reportedly considering a counteroffer involving additional crickets.

BEST CHILD(REN)'S COSTUME(S)

Winner: Entry Submitted by Ashlyn Bianchi | $50 Amazon Gift Card

There is a new sheriff in town! And this tiny Woody came prepared with a cowboy hat, a badge, and enough personality to take over the entire toy box.

From a heartwarming intergenerational cowboy moment to a sweet encounter with Minnie Mouse, this entry had our undead judges briefly remembering what it feels like to have a pulse.

Runners-Up: Entries Submitted by Christine Hart and Jacob Mickelberg

Christine Hart brought us The Sting Operation, a pint-sized creature with enough extra limbs to make even our zombie judges back away, and Lady Liberty, Junior Edition, proving freedom shines brightest when the torch is Halloween-ready.

Jacob Mickelberg's Mythic Family Adventure added a full cast of larger-than-life characters and a costume setup that clearly did not happen five minutes before trick-or-treating.

We salute all three entries. Their imagination has officially outpaced our ability to keep track of our judges' missing body parts.

UNOFFICIAL BONUS SPOTLIGHTS

BUT WAIT... THERE IS MORE!

Just when our zombie judges thought they could return to their graves, a few entries caught our attention and refused to stay buried.

They were too good to ignore, so we are giving them two unofficial special recognitions. No additional prizes, no recounts, and no guarantee these will not become official categories next year.

BLACK CAT HONORABLE MENTIONS

Entries Submitted by Lilian Shari and Weronika Stelmasiewicz

Black cats have been blamed for bad luck for centuries. After reviewing these two submissions, we suspect the real bad luck belongs to whoever tried to put them in costumes.

Midnight Bat, submitted by Lilian Shari, brings mysterious wings and a look that says “this was not in my contract.” Jester Cat, submitted by Weronika Stelmasiewicz, is clearly plotting a prank of epic proportions.

Both deserve a place in our haunted hall of fame. Their humans may wish to sleep with one eye open.

BEST OSTEOPATHIC-THEMED COSTUME

Entry Submitted by Jill Anderson

A.T. Still After Dark!

Some Halloween costumes make history. This one brought history back from the dead, complete with a distinguished beard, period attire, and a bone worthy of an osteopathic anatomy lesson.

At last, a costume that combines professional pride with a healthy amount of orthopedic... er, osteopathic humor. Our judges would applaud, but one of them misplaced a humerus.

This is a special recognition, not an additional prize category. Still, we think A.T. Still would approve.

THE HORROR LIVES ON!

A monstrous round of applause to every winner, runner-up, honorary black cat, costumed historian, and contestant who helped make our third annual Hall-O-Advocacy such a screaming success!

TO OUR WINNERS: CHECK YOUR INBOXES... IF YOU DARE!

OPAC will contact each of our six official category winners directly with instructions to claim their Amazon gift cards. No need to summon a séance, consult a crystal ball, or stalk the mail carrier.

We are, of course, assuming you provided us with a working email address! Our zombie judges may have risen from the grave, but even they can’t resurrect a bounced email. Be sure to check your spam folder, too. Apparently, even good news can end up in the crypt!

The candy may be gone and the pumpkins may be collapsing, but the costumes will live forever on the OPAC Halloween Wall of Fame.

And remember: ghosts are frightening. Zombies are unsettling. But you cannot scare us. We have seen the Medicare Physician Fee Schedule.

Happy Hall-O-Advocacy!
