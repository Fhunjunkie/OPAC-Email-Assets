AOIA-OPAC is a federally registered PAC supporting federal candidates and committees for the advancement of the osteopathic profession. Contributions are voluntary and cannot be solicited through coercion, financial reprisal, or any form of discrimination. Corporate contributions, including those from office accounts, cannot be given directly to candidates or committees. Checks must be signed by the member; otherwise, those signed by a non-member (e.g., a spouse) will be directed to the administrative account for political education purposes. AOIA-OPAC may use corporate contributions for education, fundraising, and administrative costs as allowed by law. Contributions are not tax-deductible. Federal law requires OPAC to collect the name, address, occupation, and employer of donors contributing over $200 in a calendar year.
